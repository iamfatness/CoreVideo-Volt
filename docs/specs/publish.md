# Publish

Status: after a span can be approved. Before the clock.

## Job

One approved span becomes a house file, an OTT package, and a social cut. Each is a version on the same asset. URLs and as-run write back.

## In scope

- Approve a span. Refuses a span that is not cleared.
- Three workers: house mezzanine, HLS ladder, 9:16 with burned captions.
- Loudness and caption burn via an engine. We do not write the meter or the muxer.
- Version graph. Replay does not mint an asset.
- Write the published URL onto the version.

## Out of scope

IMF. DRM. A social network's native editor. Aspera or Signiant as a product. Broadcast delivery to a network origination desk.

## Acceptance

- One button, three versions, one asset ID.
- A restricted span cannot publish.
- A failed ladder retry does not duplicate the version row.
- The rundown item shows the URL after publish.

## Depends on

Library span. Rights. FFmpeg or an external transcoder.
