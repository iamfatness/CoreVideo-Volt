    Show screen
    Space or TTAKE the next item with its cues. Space presses the focused control instead while a button or link has focus; press Esc to drop focus. Ignored when held down, when typing in a field, within 1.5 s of the last take, or while a request is running.

    ↑ ↓Move between rundown rows.
    SSkip the selected row.
    XKill the selected row. Press X again within 4 s to confirm.
    TabBuilding the show (new item, rename, clip, cue) is by form and button, with no single-key shortcut. Space types in a field and never takes.
    Scripts screen only
    Ctrl+SSave the story, only while the cursor is inside the script box. Anywhere else it does nothing. Space and the other Show keys never act on this screen.
    Library screen only
    KPlay or pause the proxy; stops a shuttle.
    J LShuttle: reverse and forward. Press again for 2x, 4x, 8x.
    , .Step one frame back or forward.
    [ ]Jump to the previous or next shot.
    I OMark in or out at the playhead.
    EnterAdd a clip from the marks, when no button or field has focus.
    SpaceOn a row's name: add or remove the row from the selection. Enter on the name seeks the player. Space does not take on this screen.
    TabIngest a file: type the path in the Ingest field (Space types) and press Enter. No single-key shortcut, and Space does not take on this screen.
    Library, Find tab
    /Go to Find and focus the search field (from the Library screen, when no field has focus).
    ↑ ↓Move between results. Enter opens the asset, and a speech hit also seeks the player to it.
    EscIn the search field: clear it and search again.
    Library, Mark from transcript tab
    ← →Move the word cursor. Shift extends the range. Focus the transcript first (Tab).
    ↑ ↓Jump to the previous or next sentence. Shift extends.
    SpacePick the word under the cursor as a one-word range, or drop it. Space does not take on this screen.
    EnterAdd a clip from the selected words, with the handles and snap choice beside them.
    EscClear the selection; with nothing selected, leave the transcript.
    Library, Selection to export tab
